What is a Period?

Every month, your uterus makes a soft, plush cushion of tissue and blood just in case an egg is fertilized. When it isn't needed, your body gently sheds this cushion. It's not a wound or a cut—it's your body's natural housekeeping!

The 4 Cycle Phases

Your cycle isn't just the days you bleed—it's an entire 24–35 day rhythm guided by your body's superpowers.

1. Menstrual
Days 1–5 Cozy rest, bleeding, low energy.
2. Follicular
Days 6–13 Rising stamina, curiosity, fresh vibes.
3. Ovulatory
Day ~14 Peak confidence, glowing, outgoing.
4. Luteal
Days 15–28 Winding down, nest building, food cravings.

What Your First Period Feels Like

Movies make it look dramatic, but usually it starts with subtle hints. Here is what is 100% normal:

  • check_circle
    Brown or rusty spotting Blood turns brownish when it takes longer to travel. Completely harmless and very typical!
  • check_circle
    Mild low belly warmth or dull aches Your uterus muscles squeeze gently to shed the lining. Feels like a full or tight tummy.
  • check_circle
    Surprise timing It might start with just a few drops in your underwear. You won't bleed through everything instantly.

Soothing Cramps & Self-Care

Cramps happen when uterus muscles contract. Treat yourself gently with these easy remedies:

local_fire_department Gentle Warmth A hot water bottle or warm bath relaxes tense pelvic muscles immediately.
hotel Child's Pose Rest on knees with forehead on mattress to relieve lower back pressure.
emoji_food_beverage Warm Chamomile Tea Naturally calms spasms and hydrates your hard-working cells.

When to Talk to a Doctor

Most period experiences are standard body growing pains. However, never hesitate to ask a doctor, school nurse, or parent if you notice:

info
Very heavy flow Completely soaking through more than one pad an hour for multiple hours in a row.
