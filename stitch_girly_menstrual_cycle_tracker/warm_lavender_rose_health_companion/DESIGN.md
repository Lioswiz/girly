## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a soothing, private, and body-positive sanctuary. Designed for menstrual cycle tracking, symptom logging, and reproductive health literacy, the atmosphere prioritizes reassurance, gentleness, and scientific clarity without feeling clinical or infantilizing. 

The aesthetic merges **soft tactile minimalism** with **diffused luminous glass accents**. Visuals avoid harsh, clinical starkness in favor of creamy organic surfaces, delicate illuminated gradient borders, pill-shaped markers, and deeply humanized micro-interactions. The emotional goal is immediate de-stressing, complete privacy confidence, and an intuitive daily rhythm that feels like an empathetic personal journal.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in comforting botanicals, berry tones, and calming lavenders balanced against warm cream backdrops:

- **Primary (`#D15B78`) — Blush Berry / Soft Rose:** Represents the menstrual phase, key calls to action, and vital cyclical touchpoints. It balances warmth with high contrast legibility.
- **Secondary (`#8A6CB5`) — Soothing Lilac / Lavender:** Signifies the fertile window, gentle education highlights, and supportive micro-badges.
- **Tertiary (`#5A2A55`) — Deep Plum:** Anchor tone for elevated editorial headers, active high-emphasis containers, and evening wellness contexts.
- **Neutral (`#FBF8F5`) — Clean Neutral Cream:** Base backdrop replacing cold pure whites to reduce eye strain and establish a serene, warm canvas.

### Phase Badges & Functional Accents
- **Period Phase:** Soft blush rose background (`#FCEEF2`) with deep berry text (`#B83A5D`).
- **Fertile Window:** Warm lilac wash (`#F3EEF9`) with violet-lavender text (`#7352A3`).
- **Ovulation Peak:** Glowing luminous purple tint (`#EADBFC`) with plum-violet text (`#5E2E91`) paired with a subtle inner glow.
- **Luteal / Free Window:** Pale calm mint/slate wash (`#EEF6F4`) with serene eucalyptus slate text (`#396B63`).

### Dark Mode
When switching to dark mode, surfaces shift from cream to midnight plum-charcoal (`#1A141E`), retaining luminous pastel accents with lower saturation and elevated glow filters to maintain softness and avoid harsh contrast shocks.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans** for its geometric clarity, friendly open counters, and gentle terminals. The structure conveys warmth and accessibility while upholding high typographic hierarchy for cycle timelines and health insights.

- **Display & Large Headlines:** Used for daily cycle countdowns ("Day 14: Ovulation Approaching") and celebration milestones. Uses tighter letter spacing for cohesive impact.
- **Body:** Kept airy with a generous 1.5x to 1.6x line-height ratio to make symptom logs, body literacy articles, and sensitive educational content effortless to digest.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Weighted medium to semibold with slight positive tracking to ensure phase labels, mood tags, and dates are quickly scannable on smaller mobile screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around mobile-first utility, using an 8pt baseline rhythm with 4pt sub-steps for dense micro-card alignments.

- **Mobile Viewports (<600px):** Single-column fluid view with `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins, allowing cards to comfortably frame daily cycle rings, calendar strips, and symptom trackers. Horizontal carousels for horizontal logging strips feature partial edge bleed to cue scrollability.
- **Tablet / Large Screens (≥600px):** 2-column or 3-column asymmetric layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, grouping calendar/cycle prediction on the left and daily insight micro-cards and educational articles on the right. Content max-width is constrained to `840px` to maintain personal, app-like intimacy.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Generous inner component padding (`space-lg`) gives cards an uncrowded, calming presence, while tighter gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) connect related symptom tags and chips.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a layered, ambient depth model to prevent clinical harshness:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Neutral Warm Cream (`#FBF8F5`).
  - **Elevated Micro-Cards:** Pure Soft White (`#FFFFFF`) with ultra-fine translucent borders (`1px solid rgba(138, 108, 181, 0.08)`).
  - **Highlighted Cycle Hub:** Translucent layered gradient cards utilizing multi-stop radial blurs (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.9), rgba(243,238,249,0.7))`) with backdrop blur (`blur(12px)`).
- **Ambient Shadow System:**
  - **Resting Cards:** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(90, 42, 85, 0.04), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(209, 91, 120, 0.03)` — an ultra-diffused tint of plum and blush rather than cold gray.
  - **Floating / Active Modals:** `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(90, 42, 85, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(209, 91, 120, 0.06)`.
- **Glow Accents:** Ovulation and fertile highlights incorporate a faint, breathing ambient halo (`box-shadow: 0px 0px 16px 2px rgba(138, 108, 181, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic curvature to foster friendliness, comfort, and human touch:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Employ `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px) and `rounded-3xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to avoid sharp corners entirely.
- **Interactive Badges, Phase Pills & CTA Buttons:** Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create tactile, friendly targets.
- **Micro Symptom Icons & Daily Date Cells:** Smooth circular shapes or continuous squircle forms (`rounded-xl` / 12px) that feel natural under thumb taps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Log Today's Symptoms"):** Full pill (`rounded-full`), background in Blush Berry (`#D15B78`) with crisp white label typography. Micro-gradient hover state leaning towards deep rose (`#B83A5D`), with a gentle plum drop shadow.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill-shaped with a soft lilac tint (`#F3EEF9`), text in deep plum-violet (`#5A2A55`), with zero harsh borders.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Pure text in `#5A2A55` paired with a soft lavender hover state.

### Phase & Status Pill Badges
- Compact, auto-layout pills (`px-3 py-1`) with `label-sm` semibold styling.
- Distinct color-coded variants:
  - **Period:** `#FCEEF2` background, `#B83A5D` text, subtle rose dot indicator.
  - **Fertile:** `#F3EEF9` background, `#7352A3` text, soft lavender dot indicator.
  - **Ovulation:** Gradient `#F3EEF9` to `#EADBFC`, `#5E2E91` text, subtle luminous ring.
  - **Free / Safe Window:** `#EEF6F4` background, `#396B63` text.

### Micro-Cards & Symptom Chips
- **Interactive Chips (Mood, Flow, Cervical Mucus, Pain):** Flexible pill containers with subtle cream borders. Inactive states are white with muted plum text; selected states transition to rich lilac or berry with an inner soft tint and gentle spring scale animation.
- **Daily Insight Micro-Cards:** White background with delicate 1px gradient borders. Divided into clear vertical tiers: icon/pill header, concise observation in `headline-sm`, and reassuring educational context in `body-sm`.

### Calendar Representations
- **Horizontal 7-Day Quick Strip:** Compact cards for daily check-ins. Today's date is framed by a soft berry ring; cycle phases are denoted by delicate underline bars or colored glowing dots beneath each date.
- **Monthly Phase Grid:** Circular day nodes sitting within card containers. Shaded color fills bridge across days in the same phase to form an unbroken, continuous visual ribbon.

### Input Fields & Sliders
- Text and numerical inputs feature floating labels, generous interior padding (`12px 16px`), and rounded-xl corners (`12px`).
- Border shifts from a faint lilac tint (`rgba(138, 108, 181, 0.2)`) to an illuminated berry glow (`#D15B78`) on focus, accompanied by an ambient focus ring.
